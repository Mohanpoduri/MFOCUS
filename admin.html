How To Deploy

Your photos are stored in this browser's local storage. To share your portfolio with others:

  1. Upload photos here in the admin panel
  2. Push index.html + admin.html + vercel.json to GitHub
  3. Deploy on Vercel — it's free
  4. Visit your live admin URL and upload photos there (so they're stored on that domain)
  5. Share your Vercel URL — visitors see everything instantly ✓

⚠ Important: Upload from your deployed Vercel URL, not from local files. Photos saved here are only visible in this browser.
